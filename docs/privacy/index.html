Privacy Policy

easemo is a local-first macOS app. Your screen recordings, webcam video, microphone audio, and exported videos stay on your Mac unless you choose to share them yourself.

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Data collection

easemo does not collect personal data, does not require an account, and does not include analytics or tracking.

Local recordings

Recordings are created and processed locally on your Mac. easemo does not upload your recordings to a cloud service.

Permissions

easemo asks macOS for screen recording, camera, and microphone access so you can capture demos. You can manage these permissions in macOS System Settings.

AI processing

easemo does not use AI processing and does not send recordings to AI services.

Files you export

Exported MP4 files are saved to the location you choose. You control where those files are stored and whether they are shared.

Contact

For privacy questions, email lbox@novs.uk or create an issue on GitHub.
